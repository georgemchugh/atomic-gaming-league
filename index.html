What AGL is

Online-first competitive community. We run a WARDOGS community server and house-rules cups from day one of Early Access, and we write the ruleset in public before bigger seasons.

  • WARDOGS Flagship. Community server + house-rules cups first.
  • StarCraft II On the AGL slate for later ranked nights and the festival harvest.

House rules

Play is loose. These four are not.

  • No harassment, excessive trolling, or bullying.
  • No artillery, vehicles, or explosives on an opponent MAIN BASE. One warning, then you are out of the night.
  • Hacking or cheating = instant permanent ban.
  • AGL-listed matches: name on the cup roster.

External command voice is strongly encouraged, not required. In-game prox is public. Enemies hear it.
