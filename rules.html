House rules · Pacific Time

Play is loose.
These four are not.

Community cup server. Not an official BULKHEAD or Team17 circuit. Field manual comes later.

The four hard rules

  1. No harassment. No harassment, excessive trolling, or bullying. One night, one community. Staff decide the line.
  2. No fire on a MAIN BASE. No artillery, vehicles, or explosives on an opponent main base. One warning, then you are out of the night.
  3. No cheats. Hacking or cheating is an instant permanent ban.
  4. Listed matches need a roster name. If AGL lists the match, your Steam name and Discord are on the cup roster first. That is a roster, not a license.

What a main base is

Main base = HQ / spawn / vendor pad. A FOB is not automatically a main base. HQ footprints in WARDOGS are still being mapped. If you are not sure, do not put indirect fire on it.

Voice

External command voice is strongly encouraged, not required. In-game proximity chat is public. Enemies hear it. Do not put callouts you cannot afford to leak on prox.

How we run a night

Pubs most nights. Cups when three captains are ready. Referees can sit a player for the night. Permanent bans are for cheats and for people who come back after a sit and do the same thing.

Full field manual ships later at /manual. This page is the public short rules.
